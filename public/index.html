Phishing Awareness Lab

Practice spotting suspicious login flows without copying a real brand or collecting real credentials. This demo accepts a training identifier, immediately discards the password field, and explains what defenders should inspect.

Educational use only: do not enter a real password. The server is designed to discard it.

Defender checklist

  • Verify the URL and domain before entering sensitive information.
  • Use a password manager; it usually will not autofill on look-alike domains.
  • Report suspicious pages to your security team instead of interacting further.
  • Prefer phishing-resistant MFA such as security keys or passkeys.
